Privacy Policy — Harbor

Harbor for Android (app.harborsynth) · Last updated: 20 October 2026

Harbor is an app that plays M-VAVE FM-1 firmwares on Android phones and tablets. This policy covers the Harbor app. The in-app summary (About & licences ▸ Privacy) says the same in brief.

What Harbor collects

Nothing. Harbor has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it doesn't send information about you or your device to its developer or to anyone else.

Network use

Harbor connects to the internet only to:

  • fetch the list of firmwares from fm1-emu's site (joakimeriksson.github.io) and from the FM-1 Firmware Hub (thefirmwarehub.com), at most once a day or when you refresh the list, and
  • download a firmware when you choose to play it, from the Firmware Hub or from where its author publishes it.

These are ordinary web requests: the servers receive your IP address and the request, as with any download, under their own privacy policies. Harbor adds nothing about you to them.

Links you tap ("Source", "On the Hub", "Download from M-VAVE") open in your browser.

What stays on your device

Downloaded firmwares, what each firmware saves (its flash memory), and your settings are kept in Harbor's private storage. They leave the device only:

  • when you export or share a backup (.fm1save), to the place or app you choose, and
  • through Android's own backup, if you have it turned on: settings and saves are included, firmware packages are not.

Uninstalling Harbor deletes all of it.

Recordings you make are saved as WAV files in the shared Music/Harbor folder, where your music and file apps can see them. Harbor sends them nowhere; they leave the device only if you share them (Harbor's Share button opens Android's share sheet) or move them yourself. Uninstalling Harbor doesn't delete them.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan and connect): asked for only when you pair a Bluetooth MIDI instrument, and used only to find and connect to it. Harbor declares that it never uses Bluetooth to derive your location.
  • Notifications: asked for when you turn on "Keep playing in background", to show the playback notification.
  • Internet: for the downloads above.

Recording needs no permission: Android lets an app save its own audio files to the Music folder. Harbor doesn't use the microphone.

MIDI from USB instruments, Bluetooth instruments, a computer or other apps is processed on the device and goes nowhere else. Harbor sends no MIDI out.

Diagnostics

"Copy a report" (Audio & performance) puts your device's model, Android version and Harbor's recent diagnostic log on your clipboard, only when you tap it, so you can choose to send it to someone. Harbor itself sends it nowhere.

Children

Harbor isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
